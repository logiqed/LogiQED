# LogiQED Vision

LogiQED is not a freight marketplace. It is a cryptographic evidence layer for physical logistics.

## The Problem

Freight disputes are expensive and trust-heavy. When a truck is late, the reason is unclear. Traffic? Weather? Warehouse queue? Driver mistake? Everyone has their own version.

Carriers wait months for settlements. Drivers face unfair fines. Insurers process PDFs and phone calls. Shippers get promises, not proof.

## The Solution

LogiQED converts telemetry and trip events into verifiable Evidence Packages.

The chain:

Sensor/device → attestation → timestamp → signature → provenance → ZK → Evidence Package.

Each package states what happened, why it happened, and which SLA rule applies. Responsibility is confirmed or excluded based on verifiable data.

## The Product

LogiQED is an Evidence Operating System for logistics.

1. **INGEST** — physical events become authenticated digital events.
2. **PROVENANCE** — signed event stream becomes Evidence Graph.
3. **DECISION** — SLA rules become Claims.
4. **PROOF** — Claims become cryptographic Evidence Packages.

## For Whom

- **Drivers** — SLA protects, not punishes. Traffic, breakdown, weather auto-recalculated.
- **Carriers** — disputes close in minutes, not months.
- **Insurers** — Evidence Package instead of PDFs.
- **Shippers** — proof of what happened, not promises.

## Why LogiQED

- **Evidence, not marketplace.** Marketplace comes later. Trust graph first.
- **Graded confidence.** Trust Levels E0–E5. Not binary trust.
- **Targeted ZK.** One or two high-value claims first. No overengineering.
- **Privacy-by-design.** Hashes and proofs, not raw telemetry forever.
- **Crypto-agile.** Ed25519 + ML-DSA. Post-quantum ready.
- **Regulatory tailwind.** EU eFTI Regulation from July 9, 2027.

## Vision

Become the standard evidence layer for physical logistics. Then expand to insurance, compliance, payments, marketplace, and autonomy.

Not a new Uber Freight. A cryptographic infrastructure for the global freight market.
