# Business Model

## Revenue Model

LogiQED monetizes verifiable freight evidence.

- Platform fee per carrier or shipper
- Fee per Evidence Package
- Fee per SLA claim verification
- Enterprise API access

## Why This Works

The value grows with the volume of verified freight events.

Not with the number of UI users.

## Customers

Carriers.

Shippers.

Freight forwarders.

Insurers.

Compliance teams.

## Examples

A carrier with 1,000 trips per month.

Platform fee plus per Evidence Package.

Each SLA dispute closed by evidence saves time and legal cost.

## Long-Term

eFTI compliance tooling.

Insurance API integrations.

Marketplace on top of trust graph.

## Principle

Verifiable freight infrastructure scales with freight volume, not with users.
