# DePIN Integrations

## Idea

Connect driver devices and sensors to DePIN networks.

## Examples

- Hivemapper: road mapping
- DIMO: vehicle telemetry
- WindChain: wind data
- SignalMap: connectivity data

## Value

Drivers earn from data collected during trips.

## Why Later

Requires stable device onboarding and token incentives.

Core evidence layer must be production first.
