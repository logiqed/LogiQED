# Warehouse Marketplace

## Idea

Flexible warehouse space leasing.

## Problem

Long-term leases create risk and inefficiency.

## How It Works

1. Warehouse operators list available slots.
2. Shippers book short-term capacity.
3. Access and state verified by evidence.
4. Payments settle via smart contract.

## Technical Dependencies

- Warehouse identity
- Slot management
- Measurement Boundary for warehouse state
- Smart contract settlement

## Why Later

Needs warehouse onboarding and legal model.

Evidence layer first.
