# Soulbound Reputation

## Idea

Non-transferable reputation for drivers, carriers and warehouses.

## Problem

Reputation is fragmented and easy to fake.

## How It Works

1. Every action produces signed evidence.
2. Evidence accumulates in Evidence Graph.
3. Reputation score derived from verified history.
4. Score stored as soulbound credential.
5. Marketplace and partners use score for decisions.

## Reputation Sources

- On-time delivery
- SLA compliance
- Safety events
- Warehouse feedback
- Sensor data quality

## Technical Dependencies

- Evidence Graph
- Trust Levels
- Smart contracts
- Identity layer

## Why Later

Reputation becomes valuable after network usage.

First prove evidence layer works.
